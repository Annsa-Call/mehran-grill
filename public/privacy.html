Your table. Your data.

Mehran Grill collects your name and Pakistani mobile number to process orders, match repeat visits and provide your loyalty card. Marketing is optional and off by default. Birthday day and month are optional; a birth year is not collected.

How it works

A qualifying visit is an order marked completed and paid by authorized staff. A cookie links your card securely to this browser. Cart contents may be kept on your device; phone numbers are not stored in browser local storage.

Restaurant staff can access orders and customer records. Cloudflare processes application and database data. When official WhatsApp messaging is enabled, Meta processes the phone number and message. No payment card details are collected by this app.

Your choices

Manage consent in My rewards or reply STOP to a connected WhatsApp campaign. Ask restaurant staff to export or delete your record, or to unlink a lost device. Deleting a customer also deletes their associated orders and loyalty history. The restaurant must set its retention period and statutory record-keeping policy before live operation.

Demo mode contains synthetic customers and orders only. No live marketing messages are sent from demo mode.

Rewards never depend on leaving a review. The app records review-link clicks, not whether Google accepted a review.
